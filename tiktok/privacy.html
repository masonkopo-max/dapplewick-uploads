Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Carousel Drafts is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok accounts it connects to. It uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to send photo carousels the owner made to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app. The tool never posts anything publicly.

  • The only TikTok data it uses is the signed-in account's basic profile (to confirm which account is connected) and the permission to upload drafts.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Images are hosted briefly at a temporary address so TikTok can fetch them, and are deleted once TikTok has received them.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/masonkopo-max/dapplewick-uploads.
