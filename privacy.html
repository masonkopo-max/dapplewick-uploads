Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Dapplewick Uploads is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it manages. It uses Google sign-in and the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule videos, read comments on those channels' videos, and post replies.

  • The only Google account data it uses is the signed-in owner's YouTube channel data, and only to perform those actions.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Public comments it reads are used only to decide on a reply and are not stored beyond a nightly log on the owner's computer.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
